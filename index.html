Hagood Hug's

Everyone needs a hug now and then. Hagood’s Hugs is a program designed to help in times of need. I have found that students cannot focus on their schoolwork if they have major things on their minds. My program would like to fill in the gaps to help students in areas of need so that they can focus on their academics. Hagood’s Hugs would connect families with community resources to meet these needs. For example, students who have hygiene issues and are unable to get their hair done, we would partner with companies to provide products and services to help. For families struggling to get food, we would set up transportation to food banks and help apply for government assistance. At our headquarters, we would accept clothing donations so families can shop for clothes. We will also host neurodivergent friendship parties to give caregivers and students with disabilities a platform to build their community and find friends.
